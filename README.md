# ⏱ OT Tracker — Overtime & Attendance Monitor

A personal web app for tracking daily overtime and attendance. Built for employees in the Philippines who want a simple, no-fuss way to monitor their work hours and overtime balance without relying on spreadsheets or paper logs.

---

## What It Does

### 📋 Dashboard
Your main workspace. Every workday, a new attendance record is automatically created for you. When you're done for the day, hit **Time Out** and the app instantly calculates how many hours you worked and how much overtime you accumulated. A live OT banner appears at the top of the screen the moment you're in overtime, so you always know where you stand.

### 📊 Analytics
Visual charts that show your overtime and attendance trends over time. Filter by today, this week, this month, or this year. See how many days you were present, absent, or pending — all in one glance.

### ⚙️ Settings
Set your official work schedule — start time, end time, working days, and a grace period before overtime kicks in. You can also override the schedule for a specific day without changing your global settings.

---

## Why It's Useful

Most employees don't have an easy way to track their own overtime independently. OT Tracker gives you a personal record of every workday so you're never in the dark about:

- **How much OT you've accumulated** — tracked down to the minute
- **Whether you've used your OT as leave** — mark it as used and see your remaining balance
- **Your attendance history** — know exactly how many absences you have on record
- **Your work patterns** — the analytics page shows trends so you can spot months where you consistently overwork

It runs entirely in the browser, works offline, and syncs automatically when you're back online — so it's reliable even with an unstable internet connection.

---

## Who It's For

Any employee on a fixed work schedule who wants to:
- Keep their own honest record of overtime hours
- Track whether their OT has been converted to leave
- Have proof of attendance and work hours independent of their employer's system
